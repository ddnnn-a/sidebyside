Why SideBySide

A strip that stays yours

Pick an edge — right, left, top, or bottom — and SideBySide keeps that area reserved. Other windows are gently pushed back into the work area, so your docked apps are never covered.

One shortcut in, one out

Press ⌃⌥⌘ → to dock the frontmost window, ⌃⌥⌘ ← to release it. Docked windows split the strip evenly, automatically. Drag & drop works too.

Per-display control

Each display gets its own strip, edge, and size (10–50%). Settings persist across reconnects — built for multi-monitor setups.

Native and lightweight

A single menu bar utility written in Swift. No Electron, no background bloat, no network calls while you work. macOS 14 or later.

How it works

  1. Buy and install. After checkout your license activates automatically — no key to copy and paste.
  2. Grant accessibility permission. SideBySide moves and resizes other apps' windows, which macOS gates behind one checkbox in System Settings.
  3. Dock your first window. Bring Slack to the front, press ⌃⌥⌘ →, and it snaps into the strip. Add more and they share the space evenly.

FAQ

Which macOS versions are supported?
macOS 14 Sonoma and later, on Apple silicon and Intel. The app is notarized by Apple.
Why does it need accessibility permission?
Arranging other apps' windows is only possible through macOS's accessibility API. The permission is used for moving and resizing windows — nothing else.
How do I move my license to a new Mac?
A license covers 3 active Macs. You can deactivate a device any time from your purchase portal.
